# Three minutes, one maker, one decision

This script is a presentation aid. Enter the requests live and wait for actual results; timings depend on the model. Do not claim a prerecorded result is live.

**0:00–0:20 — The person and problem**
“I’m a keyboard maker. I like this case and want to make the keyboard wireless. Today that means reading CAD and component documents separately, then discovering the incompatibility after choosing the parts. Flare lets me have that conversation with the design.”

Open the source keyboard. Point to the imported case and individually selectable parts.

**0:20–0:45 — Give the model evidence**
Open Design notebook. Show the source files and one current limit. Add a real datasheet or BOM if it is not already loaded. Show a PDF page and make clear which uploaded numerical facts need review.

**0:45–1:15 — Make the first change**
“Make it wireless. Keep the original case and USB charging. Use normal 100 mA charging.”

The live model proposes parts. Point to the checked revision and the proposed envelopes inside the source enclosure. There is no apply until the final proposal passes the listed checks.

**1:15–2:05 — Show the reason this matters**
“Switch to at least 500 mA charging. Keep the battery under 4 mm tall and keep the same case.”

Inspect actual attempts. In the checked catalog, the thin cell’s 150 mA limit conflicts with 500 mA charging; the larger 500 mAh cell violates the height requirement. Click the excerpt. The useful output is a source-backed refusal to approve a conflicting design, not an attractive unchecked model.

**2:05–2:35 — Resolve the conflict**
“Use normal 100 mA charging. Keep the battery under 4 mm tall and keep the other constraints.”

When the real checks pass, apply. Undo shows that the entire edit is reversible.

**2:35–3:00 — Show the multimodal loop and close**
Inspect this view with Gemini. The actual render is sent with the documents. Explain that visual observations complement numeric tools and cannot certify hardware. If the call is still running, state that honestly; do not fake completion.

“Flare brings the assembly, documents and the change into one inspectable conversation. This first workflow helps makers catch a documented conflict before they build. It is a checked design study, not physical hardware certification.”

For judging, describe full-document reasoning, multimodal input and tool feedback as implemented capabilities. Claims that the result is only possible with Argon, faster than a smaller model, or useful to interviewed makers require additional measured evidence.
