# Etch component models

Copied from `PranavAchar01/etch`, commit `c548129ec5ed13f59f76b0a852a5dd03b728825b`, `deploy/etch-app/parts3d/`. Upstream assets are exported from KiCad STEP models; `index.json` preserves the footprint identity and original millimetre bounding boxes. Imported at the user’s explicit direction and with their stated permission. See the app’s third-party notices for attribution.

Only exact catalog footprints are used for whole parts. ESP32-WROOM-32 is used as the module on the DevKit, not substituted for the complete development board. DHT11 and unrelated stand-ins are intentionally excluded.
